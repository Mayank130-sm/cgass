Project Title
FlavorGraph: Understanding and Generating Recipes with ML
Description
This project applies machine learning and network analysis to computational gastronomy. We build a knowledge graph linking recipes, ingredients, cuisines and flavor compounds, and use it to study food-pairing patterns across cuisines. We then train models for cuisine prediction and ingredient substitution, and combine everything into an interactive web app that also generates and scores novel recipe ideas.
Deliverable 1
Recipe and Flavor Knowledge Graph with Analysis: a cleaned dataset of recipes, ingredients, cuisines and flavor compounds, built into a weighted ingredient graph. It includes an analysis of food-pairing patterns, ingredient communities and cuisine similarity, with interactive network visualisations.
Deliverable 2
ML Models for Cuisine Prediction and Ingredient Substitution: a cuisine classifier (TF-IDF + logistic regression baseline, compared against gradient boosting or a neural network) and an embedding-based ingredient substitution model (Word2Vec/Node2Vec). Both are evaluated, using accuracy and macro-F1 for classification and a small human rating study for substitutions.
Deliverable 3
FlavorGraph Web App with Recipe Generation: an interactive Streamlit/Flask app where users enter ingredients to get a predicted cuisine and substitution suggestions. It also generates novel ingredient combinations or recipe ideas, ranked by a flavor-compatibility score derived from the knowledge graph.
